# BRIEF: Field, a farming year under one line

**Self-authored under explicit creative delegation.** Nothing here is a user quotation; every answer is an authored decision. Evidence used: `apps/client/src/pages/atlas/domains/content/field.ts` (all copy, the five crop rows with from/to months, three grower stories, four proof figures, the form), `base.ts` (name, statement, CTA label from the last item of `tree`: "Grow with us"), `type.ts` (Bitter + Source Sans 3; Noto Naskh Arabic), the domain palette (paper #f3f3e5, ink #24351f, one flat accent #a5c957), and stills `field-1..4.webp` (bare furrows to sprouts). The hero film is not used. Everything on the page is sample content and says so on its face.

## The eight topics

1. **Vibe.** Grounded, fertile, exact. References: a printed seed-sowing calendar, a field notebook with pencilled rows, the way a Gantt chart looks when a gardener draws it.
2. **Journey.** The visitor lands on the first crop (faba beans) already framed with its sow and harvest months, scrolls January to December under one line, then reads three growers, then plans their own sowing.
3. **Energy curve.** Calm, steady forward motion, one long swell, a quiet board, a resolved close.
4. **Feeling curve and the one moment.** Below. The moment: the whole year has passed and the cover rye is the only crop still standing.
5. **What no other site does.** Scroll is the season. One vertical line sweeps the calendar and every crop card changes stage as it is crossed, with its photograph graded to match.
6. **Range.** Editorial and warm, flat, with the density of a working calendar. No gradients, no glow.
7. **One world or scenes.** Three distinct scenes (the year, the board, the plan) joined by the fixed month bar.
8. **Assets.** The project's own stills only. Nothing generated.

## Subject and CTA

Field is a network of growers who farm for the whole field. The visitor must believe that a rotation is a calendar you can read, and that the rye left standing in December is part of the farm. One action, one label used everywhere: **Grow with us**.

## Grammar: Gallery / catalog

The crop cards are catalog objects with one label schema (name, sow and harvest months, stage, note). Why the other seven lost: filmic needs footage the project lacks; chaptered editorial would have been a third text-first page; live surface and split stage are already taken by other domains; poster and cutlist would hide the calendar; continuous world has no flight assets. Differs from `arc` (left index, lateral object pan, drawing) and `tactile` (bottom strip, vertical wall, temperature grade) on nav, hero, close and signature.

## The peak

*"I scrolled a whole farming year under one line, and the rye was the last thing left standing."* Act 1, December hold (the last tenth of the pan is parked on Dec with the line at the far edge). Act 1 is the largest span on the page; the act before the peak is the walk through summer.

## Tell-someone sentence

It's the site where you scroll a farming year and watch every crop sow, grow and get harvested under one line until only the rye is left.

## Authored decisions to note

- Stage rule (computed, not content): sow for the first quarter of a crop's months, harvest for its last 18% (min half a month), done after. A crop whose bar reaches December (the rye) is never harvested within the year: it stands. This follows the content note "left standing through winter".
- Form mapping (authored, content has no mapping): Cereals = spring oats + cover rye; Pulses and beans = faba beans; Oilseeds = sunflowers; Vegetables and potatoes = no row in the sample calendar (says so); Mixed = all five.
- Authored silence: none long. The board holds each grower for a third of 2vh; a progress bar and a slow photo push keep it alive.
- Reduced motion: the year becomes a plain list of the five cards with their own 12-month bars; the month bar highlights which crops are in the ground and jumps to the first.
