# BRIEF: atelier, a collection that cuts past while a tape measure stamps it

**Self-authored under explicit creative delegation.** No interview. Every line is an authored decision. Evidence: `content/atelier.ts` (EN and AR copy, sizing table, all sample content), `base.ts` (statement, proof, palette: ink #1e1817, paper #f8f2ee, rouge #ce3f46, soft #f3d8d7, line #e3d3cd), `type.ts` (EN Bodoni Moda + Jost, AR Markazi Text + Noto Naskh Arabic). Assets: the 4 s hero film (hero only, poster under reduced motion) and the four stills. Nothing generated. The hero foot says on its face that brand, looks, figures and quote are sample content; the success line repeats that nothing was sent.

## The eight topics

1. **Vibe.** Loud, editorial, hard-cut. References from other media: a tailor's cutting list pinned to a wall, a tape measure pulled out across a table, a fashion-week running order.
2. **Journey.** Hero is look 03, cut to look 07 in under a viewport, then five more looks, the cloth story, the sizing argument (heading, table, the fit rule), the quote, the four facts, the invitation, the email field.
3. **Energy curve.** Fast from the first screen, loudest across the six looks, a short breath in the cloth statement, steady through sizing, quiet quote, then abrupt.
4. **Feeling curve and the ONE moment.** Below. The moment: the whole run of looks cuts past and the tape stamps each one.
5. **What no other site does.** A tape measure runs along the top; as each look cuts in, it prints a stamp with that look's measurement, read cell by cell from the sizing table further down the page.
6. **Range.** Brutal-editorial: full-bleed flat grounds (paper, ink, rouge, soft rouge), one serif, no cards.
7. **One world or distinct scenes.** Distinct scenes, fourteen of them, no pin, no dwell, no crossfade.
8. **Assets.** Owned film and four stills (look 15 and 12 and so on reuse them with different crops, as the content itself maps them). No stats beyond the sample figures.

Subject: an independent label. The visitor must believe the collection is one cloth cut many ways and that sizes were measured on bodies. One action, one label everywhere: **Join the list** (AR «انضم إلى القائمة»).

## Acts (14, heights in svh)

hero+look 03 (1.0), looks 07, 12, 15, 19, 26 (0.95 each), cloth statement (0.78), sizing heading (0.72), sizing table (1.35), fit rule (0.8), quote (0.95), facts (0.9), invitation (0.75), email (1.0).

## Stamp rule (authored)

Looks are stamped at size M, using the measurement the garment is cut around: coat, shirt, vest, knit take Bust (M = 90), trouser takes Waist (72), skirt takes Hip (98). Values are read at runtime from the cells of `#sizeTable`, never typed or converted. The same line is also in each look's copy for readers without the tape.

## The peak

What a visitor would say: *"The whole collection cut past in a few screens and a tape measure printed a measurement on every look."* Lives in acts 1 to 6 (about 5.8 of 13 screens, the longest unbroken run). The tape is in the fixed chrome, driven by page scroll, not by an act. It turns rouge for exactly that run and back to paper after. The quiet before it is the hero; the quiet after it is act 7.

## Tell-someone sentence

It's the site where a tape measure runs across the top and stamps every look with a real measurement as the collection cuts past.

## Authored silence

None needed: acts are static and cut by scrolling; the tape reports a quantised state in `data-sc-verify-state` on every scroll step.

## Why the other seven grammars lost

Filmic one-shot: one 4 s clip cannot carry fourteen cuts. Chaptered editorial and Split stage: the tone is pulse, not argument. Live surface: no state to compute. Continuous world: no assets. Typographic poster: copy is a collection, not one claim. Gallery/catalog: close, but the PLAN row asks for speed, not browsing.

## Taste

Two families per language, four flat grounds, one accent. Small text on paper is ink (rouge on paper is 4.28:1); white on rouge is 4.75:1. No eyebrow, section number, scroll cue, em dash, gradient, glow. Tick marks are an SVG pattern. Reduced motion: no marquee, no tape travel, no press; the current stamp changes in place at the notch; the film stays on its poster.
