# BRIEF: Root, ask the page a question

**Self-authored under explicit creative delegation.** No interview. Every decision below is authored, not quoted. Evidence used: `apps/client/src/pages/atlas/domains/content/root.ts` (EN and AR copy and figures, all sample content), `base.ts` (royal ink #20234a, paper #f6f4ec, marigold #e9b949, statement "Find your next question.", last tree item "Find your path"), `type.ts` (EN Literata + Public Sans, AR Noto Naskh Arabic + Almarai). Nothing generated and no stills or film used. The page says on its face that brand, dates and figures are sample content.

## The eight topics

1. **Vibe.** Curious, generous, a reading room. Two grounds that never blend (paper, royal ink), one flat marigold accent. References from other media: a catechism printed in two columns, a library catalogue card, a chat reply that arrives a beat after you stop typing.
2. **Journey.** A question types itself and a mentor answers with another question (x4, one per cohort), then the page hands you the keyboard and answers yours.
3. **Energy curve.** Quiet, four small repeating beats, then one open stop where the page waits for you.
4. **Feeling curve and the ONE moment.** Below. The moment: you type a question into the page and it answers with a cohort that starts soon.
5. **What no other site does.** Type any question (divider field or the close) and the page returns the nearest cohort and mentor, by keyword match computed only from the cohort and mentor text in the page. No synonym list, no scores, no dates computed. No word matches: it says so and shows the first cohort to start.
6. **Range.** Premium-minimal, editorial. Serif questions, plain sans for everything else. Not brutalist, not playful.
7. **One world or distinct scenes.** Neither. One held stage in two halves (Split stage); each pin is one question and its answer.
8. **Assets.** Owned: the real copy and figures in `root.ts`. Used: none of the stills or the film (the type is the image). Pairing of cohort i with mentor i follows the shared `still` index in the content.

Subject: Root is live learning rooms built around a question. The visitor must believe that a good question is a good enough way in. One action, one label everywhere: **Find your path** (AR «اعثر على طريقك»).

## The peak

What a visitor would say: *"I typed my own question into the page and it came back with a real cohort and a mentor who would ask me something in return."* Lives in act 5. Act 4 is the quiet in front of it.

## Tell-someone sentence

It's the site where you type a question into the page and it answers you with the cohort that starts next.

Phone and RTL: on phones the seam is horizontal (question above, answer below) and the field sits at the bottom. On the Arabic page everything mirrors with logical properties: the question half and the typing start on the right, the answer arrives from the left, the collapse travels to the right edge.

## Taste decisions

Two families per language. Ink ring on paper, marigold ring on ink, and the field carries both. Marigold is only a fill, a line, or text on ink. No eyebrows, section numbers, scroll cue, em dash, gradient or glow. Typing is driven by scroll position (and by time once, on load, for the hero) and by grapheme, so Arabic shaping stays whole. Reduced motion: every question printed in full, no caret, the collapse becomes a crossfade.

## Authored silence

Only the end of the close (p 0.6 and up: the proof rests, `data-sc-verify-hold` on the close). Every pin keeps changing until its answer is fully in.

## Why the other seven grammars lost (and the filmic one-shot)

The product is a question and an answer, so the comparison is the argument; a film would hide it. Chaptered editorial would read the pair one after the other. Live surface alone would lose the scripted opening, so the live part is kept as the signature only. Continuous world, typographic poster, gallery and rhythmic cutlist all need assets or a single claim this sample content does not have.

## Known weaknesses

The peak shares the 1.8vh span the plan gives every act; its room is the typed answer, not extra scroll. Arabic matching strips ال and common suffixes but cannot match a broken plural to its singular beyond shared prefixes.
