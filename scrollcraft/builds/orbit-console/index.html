    Vesper-1 mission log (sample)

    1. T-minus 04:00. Propellant load complete. Tanks topped off, all 23 flight controllers polled, and the weather officer called the upper winds acceptable.
    2. T+00:31. Separation and first signal. Vesper-1 left the upper stage on schedule and the ground station at Kourou held its carrier for the full pass.
    3. Day 3. Sunshield fully deployed. Five layers unfurled over nine hours, with the cold side dropping below 60 kelvin by evening.
    4. Day 29. Insertion burn at L2. A 41-second burn placed the observatory on its halo orbit, 1.5 million km from Earth.
    5. Day 112. First light, all four instruments. The mid-infrared camera returned its first calibrated frame, a field of 3,000 faint galaxies.
    6. Day 214. Science operations begin. Commissioning closed with 98% of the planned checks passed and the first public observing queue opened.
