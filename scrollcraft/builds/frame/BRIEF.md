# BRIEF: Frame, a light meter you read the work through

**Self-authored under explicit creative delegation.** No interview. Every decision below is authored, not quoted. Evidence used: `content/frame.ts` (EN and AR copy, figures; all sample content), `type.ts` (EN Italiana + Jost, AR El Messiri + Tajawal), `base.ts` palette (paper #1c1a19, soft #2d2927, ink/linen #f8f3ed, accent #d8c3a7, border #413a35), the 4 s hero film and `frame-1..4.webp`. Nothing generated. The page says on its face that the brand and everything on it is sample content. Source: `build.mjs` generates both pages.

## The eight topics

1. **Vibe.** Dark room, one window of light. Quiet, patient, printed. References: a darkroom enlarger timer, the edge code printed on 35mm stock, a gallery label under a hung print.
2. **Journey.** A title page with an empty frame, five series one chapter at a time, how the work is made, then the print order set under a photograph.
3. **Energy curve.** Calm, steady, one long swell, one held light.
4. **Feeling and the ONE moment.** Curve below. The moment: the page opens to full exposure and the light holds.
5. **What no other site does.** A light meter. Scroll sets the exposure: each still lifts toward the centre of the screen and falls at the edges, and an f-stop and shutter readout in the film edge follows it.
6. **Range.** Editorial and premium-minimal, dark, hard cuts. Not playful.
7. **One world or scenes.** Distinct chapters (Chaptered editorial). Grounds change by hard cut, never by fade.
8. **Assets.** Owned: the Frame hero film (scrubbed once, in the peak chapter, re-encoded for scrubbing, desktop and phone) and its four stills. No generation.

Subject: a photography practice. The visitor must believe the work is worth waiting for. One action, one label everywhere: **Commission a story** (AR «كلّفنا بقصة»).

## The peak

What a visitor would say: *"I kept scrolling and the whole page opened like a lens, then the light just stayed."* Lives in the "How the work is made" chapter, the largest span on the page (a 2.5vh scrub act, 1.5vh of travel). The five series before it are the quieter run-up.

## Tell-someone sentence

It's the site where scrolling is the exposure dial: the photographs lift and fall as you read, and at the end the whole page opens and holds the light.

## Authored silence

- Peak travel from 76% to the end of the act and the whole close hold at f/1.4, 1/2 (`data-sc-verify-hold` on the edge). The quote is read here.
- Under reduced motion the peak and close are one still linen frame, declared a hold.

## Why the other six grammars lost (and filmic one-shot)

- Filmic one-shot: one 4 s clip is not a film, and the argument is a collection.
- Split stage: no pair to compare.
- Live surface: there is no state to compute and a fake booking tool breaks the honesty rule.
- Continuous world: no long flight of assets exists.
- Typographic poster: the copy is a catalogue, not one claim.
- Gallery or catalog: closest rival, but chapters let each series have its own ground and caption; five items do not need a grid.
- Rhythmic cutlist: the tone is patience, not cuts.

## Taste decisions

Two families per language, only the weights in `type.ts`. No eyebrows, section numbers, scroll cue, em dash, gradient or glow. Flat sand accent on dark; on linen the accent appears only as fills and the ink carries the type. Every state of the stepping peak ground holds 4.5:1 or better. Focus rings are theme-matched (sand on dark, charcoal on linen).
