# BRIEF: Cinder, a typographic poster with one clock

**Self-authored under explicit creative delegation.** No interview. Evidence: `content/cinder.ts` (all copy, figures, EN and AR, sample content), `tokens.ts` (acid #e7f04f, bone #f6f6df, ink #15150f), `type.ts` (Bungee + Barlow + JetBrains Mono; Changa + Cairo). No video or stills used, no generated assets: type is the imagery. The page says on its face that Cinder is a sample brand.

## The eight topics
1. **Vibe.** Playful, dangerous, loud then hushed. References from other media: an arcade attract screen, an alarm clock radio at 03:17, a museum wall label.
2. **Journey.** Enter (WEIRD), pick a faction, two more factions, the dev log, 03:17, wishlist.
3. **Energy curve.** Loud, loud, medium, silent, loudest, quiet.
4. **Feeling curve and the ONE moment.** Below. The moment: the clock reaches 03:17 and the whole page flips.
5. **What no other site does.** Scroll is a clock. Hitting 03:17 inverts the ground to the accent and Wardens becomes Furnace. Scrolling back flips it back.
6. **Range.** Brutalist-playful poster: 30vw names, 13px small print, no images.
7. **One world or distinct scenes.** Distinct sheets on one ground that flips once.
8. **Assets.** Owned only: copy and figures. Video and stills deliberately unused.

Subject: an indie studio with one strange world and fourteen factions. Visitor must believe the weirdness is designed. One action, one label everywhere: **Wishlist the next build** (AR: أضف الإصدار القادم إلى قائمة أمنياتك).

## Why the other seven lost
Filmic one-shot and Continuous world need footage that would be one clip stretched; Chaptered editorial and Gallery would put the factions in cards; Split stage has no pair; Live surface has no real state; Rhythmic cutlist is about cuts, here the rhythm is scale.

## Taste
Two grounds only, one flat accent (acid, which becomes ink on the flipped ground). No eyebrows, counters, scroll cue, em dashes, gradients, glow or cards. Western digits in both languages, wrapped in `<bdi dir="ltr">`. Measured min text contrast 10.82:1 on the dark ground; 9.16:1 computed for soft ink on the flipped ground. Reduced motion: no wordmark distortion, no hero slide, wipes become cuts; the clock and flip still follow scroll position.
