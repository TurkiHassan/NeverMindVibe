Field guide

What our hosts tell you before you land

Short notes from the people who live along our routes. They are the reason the days feel unplanned.

Eat where the ferry crews eat

The lakeside kitchen with no menu opens at six. Ask for whatever came off the boat that morning.

Local custom · Ohrid

Take the 14:10, not the 09:30

The afternoon train has window seats on the fjord side and nobody in a hurry.

Tip · Northern Norway

Leave the ridge before sunset

The best light is on the walk down, when the pickers head home and the valley smells of woodsmoke.

Place · Rize hills

Bring something small from home

A tin of your local tea or a postcard of your street. Every host asks, and every host remembers.
