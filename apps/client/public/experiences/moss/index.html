Soil carbon gained, five of our 38 sites

Cores are drilled every spring to 30 cm and measured by an independent lab. Across all sites, recovering soil gains 3.8 times as much carbon as neighbouring unmanaged plots.

t C/ha over five yearsScale: 0 to 20

  1. Alder Fen, NorfolkRewetted peat edge with native sedge, 1,150 hectares.

    18.4 t C/ha

  2. Tabor Terraces, MoroccoStone-bunded barley and carob on slopes, 2,600 hectares.

    12.9 t C/ha

  3. Las Tres Cruces, CórdobaOlive groves under permanent cover crop, 2,100 hectares.

    9.7 t C/ha

  4. Kitui Commons, KenyaRotational grazing across eleven village herds, 2,950 hectares.

    7.2 t C/ha

  5. Dunmore Pasture, IrelandDairy fields moved to multi-species sward, 1,800 hectares.

    5.1 t C/ha

Three partners, three kinds of land

A co-op, a town and a school, in their own words about what changed after the first full season.

214Farm and community partners

  1. “The first year we paid for cover seed out of pocket. The second year the same fields held water through a six-week dry spell.”

    Hamid El IdrissiChair, Tabor Barley Co-op

    Siobhán ReillyParks officer, Dunmore Town Council
