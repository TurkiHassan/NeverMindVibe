Growers on what the field gave back

Three farms in their own words, after two or more crop cycles on our rotation plan.

  2. Mateus OkonkwoSunflower and bean grower, 85 acres
  3. Elif DanielssonBarley and potato grower, 220 acres

We undersowed our oats with clover and stopped buying nitrogen by year three. The hedgerows filled with partridge, which I had not seen since I was a boy.

Hana PetrovOat and clover grower, 140 acres

The margins around my sunflowers hum all July. My yield did not drop, and the honey from my neighbour's hives is now on my label.

Mateus OkonkwoSunflower and bean grower, 85 acres

A cover rye after harvest kept the topsoil on the slope through a wet winter. I had lost a trailer load of it every year before.
