Three questions before any line is drawn

We answer these on site, with the people who will use the building, before we open a drawing.

  • Who stands here at eight in the morning?

    We sit on the site at rush hour and draw the people before the walls.

    Weeks 1 to 3

  • Where does the light go?

    A year of sun paths becomes a plan: what is bright, what is shaded, what is allowed to be dim.

    Weeks 4 to 8

  • What will it look like after fifty winters?

    We choose materials that improve with wear and detail every joint for the repair it will need.

    Weeks 9 to 14
