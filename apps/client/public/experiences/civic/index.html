Civic, in plain text (sample content)

  • Eastgate: 9 min. Was 31 before the single repair form and the library kiosk. One door for housing repairs (2024 · Eastgate · 6 months). Five phone numbers became one form that works on paper, text message and a library kiosk.
  • Marlow Quay: 14 min. Most of the wait is still the phone queue for housing repairs. Bus stops that say where to go (2023 · Marlow Quay · 4 months). Stop signs now list the nearest clinic, school and food pantry, with the walking time to each.
  • Old Mill: 18 min. Services moved twice in a year; residents kept going to the old address. The clinic queue, made visible (2022 · Old Mill · 5 months). A wall display shows each visit's stage, so no one has to ask the receptionist where they stand.
  • Hartley Heights: 26 min. No library or service point within a 20 minute walk.
  • Canal Row: 33 min. Forms came in only two languages until the 2024 rewrite, in a ward where residents speak seven. Permit forms in seven languages (2024 · Canal Row · 8 months). Rewritten with residents at a community kitchen, then tested with a stopwatch and a cup of tea.
